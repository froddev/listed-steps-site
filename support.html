Support

Email listedsteps@mail.co.uk. This mailbox is monitored. Support and photo-correction emails, including your email address, message and attachments, are deleted 90 days after the enquiry is resolved.

Report a problem

Include the app version, phone model, Android version, the steps you followed and what happened. For a building or photo problem, include the listing name or ID and the source link where possible. The app can prepare a photo report or suggestion in your email app.

Remove private coordinates, account details and unrelated personal information from screenshots before sending.

Common questions

  • Missing photo? Photo coverage is incomplete. The building record can still be used.
  • Weather or map unavailable? Local building searches can work without those services. New photos and map backgrounds need an internet connection.
  • Location unavailable? Check Android location settings and app permissions, or enter coordinates manually.
  • Walking access? Listed status does not mean public access. The official point may not be an entrance. Follow signs and use safe, lawful routes.
  • Coverage? The current edition covers England. Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland are not yet included.
