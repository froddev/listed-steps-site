Privacy Policy · 27 October 2026

Listed Steps is an ad-free Android app for exploring listed buildings in England. You do not need an account. Contact the developer at listedsteps@mail.co.uk for privacy questions or support.

Location and weather

When you choose Use my location, Android asks for location permission. Your approximate or precise location is used to find nearby buildings and show your position. There is no background location tracking. You can decline permission and use manual coordinates. For actual or manually entered locations, coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo to request weather. Open-Meteo may keep troubleshooting logs containing IP addresses and geographical coordinates for up to 90 days, according to its published policy.

Maps, photos and internet connections

Building searches use a database stored on your phone. Map backgrounds are supplied by OpenFreeMap, using OpenStreetMap/OpenMapTiles data. Tile requests reveal the area being viewed. OpenFreeMap says its ordinary access logs omit IP addresses. Error logs may include IP addresses and requested URLs for 7 days; security-incident IP logs may be kept for up to 30 days. Its CDN provider may also process requests. Photo requests go to the image host identified by each photo source, including Wikimedia Commons and Historic England. Internet services receive connection information such as your IP address and request details, and may retain their own logs under their policies. Listed Steps does not operate a user-account or location-history server.

External apps and optional support

When you choose a Google Maps location or walking link, the destination and, when available, your starting coordinates are passed to Google Maps or your browser. Source, licence, X and Buy Me a Coffee links open external services with their own privacy policies. Financial support is optional and does not unlock app features; payment information is handled on the external service, not in Listed Steps.

Reports and email

Photo reports and suggestions create an email draft or copy text to your clipboard. Nothing is sent automatically. If you send a message, your email address, message and attachments are processed by email providers and received in the monitored support mailbox. Support and photo-correction emails, including your email address, message and attachments, are deleted 90 days after the enquiry is resolved. Include only information needed to investigate your report. Email listedsteps@mail.co.uk to ask about or request removal of correspondence.

Storage and choices

The catalogue and cached images/map data are stored on your device. Temporary screen state may include entered coordinates or report text. You can revoke location permission in Android Settings and clear app storage or uninstall to remove app-local data. Clearing app storage does not delete emails already sent or data held by external services.

Advertising and device access

This edition contains no advertising or consent SDK and no custom analytics or advertising identifier feature. It does not request access to contacts, SMS, phone identifiers, microphone or camera. Location is optional and requested in the foreground. Device location services and other platform services are governed by their providers.

This website

These privacy and support pages are prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. These pages contain no analytics, advertising, forms or embedded third-party scripts. External links open services governed by their own policies. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Updates and contact

This notice will be updated if the app changes how it handles data. Support and privacy contact: listedsteps@mail.co.uk. Please include your app version, Android version and a brief description when reporting a problem; do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.
